A protocol pause key should not live with one person. Holdfast makes containment a private two-person decision.

A detector watches one Solana vault and reconstructs the prior balance. This planted withdrawal is twenty-five percent. The policy triggers above twenty. The detector can open an incident. It cannot pause the vault.

The incident moves into a permissioned MagicBlock Ephemeral Rollup. Individual approvals stay private. One responder is never enough. Two distinct responders must approve the same stored action hash.

Quorum does not authorize an arbitrary runbook. It commits one expiring pause action against one separate vault program. Magic Actions carry that decision back to Solana.

A commit is not containment. If the pause fails, Holdfast keeps the receipt pending and says unpaused. Success requires an executed receipt and the matching paused vault.

Judges can verify the finished run without a wallet. The browser reads both deployed accounts directly from MagicBlock Devnet. Their owners, layouts, status, and incident identifiers must all agree.

Holdfast turns protocol response into a constrained, inspectable decision. Two people can stop the test drain. One person cannot.
