# Holdfast Brand Truth

## Name

Holdfast.

Not “HoldFast,” not “Hold Fast,” and not an acronym. The word is short,
physical, and already carries the right instruction: secure the thing that must
not move.

## Plain-language concept

**A watch officer’s two-key stop mechanism.**

Holdfast should feel like a piece of dependable emergency equipment maintained
by a small protocol team: quiet while watching, unmistakable when a breach is
real, and impossible for one person to operate alone. The identity combines a
ship’s watch log with the logic of a two-key interlock. It does not borrow the
visual language of generic cybersecurity shields, hacker terminals, police
badges, or crypto trading dashboards.

## Accent

Oxidized brass `#c9a86a`.

Brass signals authority, durable hardware, and keys without using loud yellow
or luxury gold. It appears on authorized controls, quorum stations, and fixed
action boundaries. It is not used as decorative confetti.

## Neutrals

- Deep blue-black `#0b0e11` — page base.
- Charcoal `#11161a` — working surface.
- Raised iron `#192027` — overlays and active inspection.
- Warm paper `#ece8df` — primary text and evidence slips.
- Weathered grey `#96938b` — secondary operational copy.

Semantic colors remain separate: muted vermilion for a verified breach, amber
for unresolved execution, and restrained green only after receipt and vault
state agree.

## Material cue

**Engraved brass set into blackened iron.**

The cue should appear through thin inset rules, precise stamped typography, and
one or two tactile controls. It must never become a skeuomorphic control-room
theme. The product remains software; the material reference makes authority and
state boundaries memorable.

## Recognition test

With the name removed, Holdfast should still be recognizable by three things:

1. A factual vertical watch log.
2. Three fixed responder stations with a two-key threshold.
3. A separate execution keel that shows commitment and containment as distinct
   facts.

## Mark constraints

- Legible at 16px.
- One-color construction before any brass treatment.
- No shield, padlock, keyhole, radar, chain link, or letter inside a hexagon.
- No Solana bars or MagicBlock logo derivative.
- Must suggest holding, bracing, or two-part mechanical agreement without
  needing an explanatory caption.
