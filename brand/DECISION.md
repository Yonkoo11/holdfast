# Holdfast brand decision

Decision date: 2026-08-09

## Concept

Approved under the user's delegated full-auto decision authority:

> A watch officer's two-key stop mechanism: oxidized brass controls set into
> blackened iron, operational rather than militaristic.

## Three marks evaluated

1. Twin Brace — rejected because the upper silhouette reads like horns at large scale.
2. Opposed Dogs — rejected because the 16px silhouette collapses toward a generic X.
3. Keel Clamp — selected because the held cross-member, opposing uprights, and
   separate lower keel remain legible at 16px and fit the product's execution model.

The selected source is `mark-3-keel-clamp.svg`. All application variants and
the favicon derive from that path; the other marks remain only as decision evidence.
